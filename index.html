Pawniac

call the piece or the move and play it!

Free chess variants, with analysis that explains your moves

Pawniac is a free chess website built around chess variants. Play classic standard chess, Hand and Brain (where one piece type is named before every move), Piece Auction (where you bid coins to build your own army) and Double Move (where each side plays two moves per turn).

There is also a bot to practise against, a game analyzer that explains your moves like a coach would, minigames, an openings quiz, leaderboards and achievements. There are no ads.
